    ⚔ CABO — Rulebook

    House Rules

    🎯 Objective

    Score the lowest hand total when the round ends. Each player starts with 4 face-down cards. Your job is to remember what you have and what you've seen, then trade or burn cards to lower your total — or call CABO when you think your hand is the lowest at the table.

    🃏 Setup

    • Each player is dealt 4 cards face-down.
    • You may briefly peek at your bottom two cards before the round begins. Memorize them.
    • Press Begin to flip them face-down. From here on, memory is your only ally — there's no persistent reveal.

    🎴 Your Turn

    1. Draw a card from the deck (drawing from the discard pile is disabled).
    2. Choose one of the following:
      • Swap it with any of your hand cards — the old card goes to discard.
      • Use its ability — only for cards 7, 8, 9, 10, J, Q, K. After the ability resolves, the drawn card is discarded.
      • Discard it with no further action.
    3. Optionally call CABO — this commits your hand for the round.
    4. Press End Turn to pass play.

    🃏 Cards & Powers

    Each card is worth its face value unless noted. Powers are only available when you draw the card, not when it's already in your hand.

    🔥 Fire

    Any player, at any time during the round, may press the 🔥 Start Fire button to interrupt the game and burn cards.

    Starting a fire

    • Select 2 or more cards sharing the same value — from any player, including yourself.
    • All selected cards are sent to the discard pile (burned).
    • If you burned cards from opponents, you must replace them: send one card from your hand to each opponent slot you burned. You choose which of your cards goes to which opponent.
    • If the selected cards don't share the same value, the burn fails — your cards stay and you draw 2 penalty cards.

    Responding to a fire

    When someone starts a fire, every other player gets a chance to respond in turn:

    • Clean burn — burn 1+ cards matching the fire's value. No penalty, cards lost cleanly.
    • New chain — burn 2+ cards of any same value (different from the current fire). This resets the response queue, and every other player gets to respond again to the new value.
    • Pass — do nothing this round.
    • Invalid burn (single non-matching card, or mismatched values) — cards stay, you draw 2 penalty cards.

    ⚠ The CABO caller's cards are frozen — they cannot be burned, peeked, or swapped after CABO has been called.

    ⚔ Calling CABO

    CABO is the "I'm ready to score" call. Use the Call CABO button at the end of your turn.

    • Every other player gets one more turn after the call.
    • Then all hands are revealed and scored.
    • Your own cards are frozen the moment you call — no one can swap, peek, or burn them until scoring.
    • +5 penalty: If you call CABO but you don't strictly have the lowest hand, your score gets an extra +5 penalty.
    • Tie-break: If you tie with another player for the lowest score, the non-caller wins — calling CABO is a risk.

    🏆 Winning

    The player with the lowest final score at round end wins. Jokers (−1) and the right strategic plays can bring your score below zero. Choose risk wisely.
