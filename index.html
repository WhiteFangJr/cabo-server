The Dark Arcanum

A Game of Hidden Knowledge & Sorcery

⚔ Objective

Have the lowest total hand score when the round ends. Jokers (🃏) are worth −1 point. Kings are the most dangerous — worth 13!

🃏 Card Values

A=1, 2–10 face value, J=11, Q=12, K=13, Joker=−1

🔮 Your Turn

  • Draw from the Deck or Discard pile
  • If from deck: Swap it into your hand, Discard it, or Use its Ability
  • If from discard: Swap or Discard only (no ability)
  • Optionally Call CABO before ending your turn

🌟 Card Abilities (deck draw only)

  • 7, 8Peek Own — Look at one of your own cards
  • 9, 10Spy — Look at an opponent's card
  • JBlind Swap — Swap a card with an opponent (unseen)
  • QSwap & Peek — Swap with opponent, then see what you got
  • KPeek & Swap — See an opponent's card, then optionally swap

⚡ CABO

Call CABO when you think you have the lowest hand. Everyone else gets ONE more turn, then the round ends. If you're not strictly the lowest scorer, you get +5 penalty!

🔥 Fire!

At ANY time, burn 2+ matching cards (yours and/or opponents'). If anyone burns wrong cards = draw 2 penalty cards. The Fire pauses the turn and resumes after!
